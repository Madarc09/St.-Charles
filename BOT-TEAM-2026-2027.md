## Selection method

Only the three completed seasons 2025–26, 2024–25 and 2023–24 supplied results, weighted 55% / 30% / 15%. The available-player list supplied identity, position and current club. The saved draft excluded all 60 owned selections. **No 2026–27 game results supplied a ranking input.**

The model estimates goals, assists, shorthanded goals and game-winning goals, then applies this pool's formula: **2G + A + 5SHG + 5GWG**. Projected NHL points alone are insufficient because the goal and bonus weights change the order.

Historical rates receive a 12-game positional prior to limit tiny-sample outliers. Expected availability blends weighted games played (65%) with a full historical season (35%), then scales to the 84-game schedule. Established players' scoring estimates blend the historical model and preseason outlook equally. Rookies with fewer than 40 NHL games use 95% of their preseason point outlook and a conservative goal/assist split. Unproven players without an outlook are not promoted by a one-game sample.

GWG estimates blend a regressed historical rate with 15% of expected goals. SHG uses a 20-game zero prior. These are modelling choices, not guaranteed predictions.

Team goalies use **2W + 5A + 10G + 5SO**. Projected wins blend 35% historical performance and 65% preseason expectations; shutouts blend 70% history and 30% a neutral prior. Every club receives the same small goalie goal/assist allowance, so rare events do not dominate selection. Team units cover whichever goalies play for that club.

The roster is the top six eligible forwards, top four defencemen and top two available team goalie units under those estimates. Rookie candidates, including Gavin McKenna, Porter Martone and Cole Hutson, were considered but did not displace the selected players. Nearby alternatives included Alex DeBrincat and Jack Hughes at forward, Brandon Montour and Thomas Harley on defence, and Edmonton/Vegas team goalies. Several differences were small; this is an informed competitive roster, not a certainty.

`docs/bot-model/selection-projections.json` records the model's own estimates and leading alternatives. Those estimates never enter the site's live statistics.

## Scoring and preservation

BOT receives the same complete regular-season scoring window as the five human teams, including games already played. Excluding current results from selection does not discard those games from its standings total.

The canonical identity-only roster is `data/bot-teams/20262027.json`; its generated browser copy is `assets/js/bot-roster-20262027.js`. There is no automatic redraft, substitution or roster editing feature. It activates only when the saved human ownership matches and the draft is locked. A conflict disables BOT without touching anyone's picks.

End Season saves its stats and selection record in `archivedSeason.botTeam`. Human standings/champion records and all original draft picks keep their existing format. BOT stays archived and does not carry over to a different season automatically.
